# Launch checklist

## Review before connecting live customers

- Complete the synthetic planner and founder flow. Confirm that registering alone never unlocks prices.
- Reconcile costs, floors, scope caps and service owners. Approve only specific offers with defensible economics.
- Confirm timezone, consultation window, buffers, holidays and response expectations.
- Configure dated FX for international priority review and keep native price catalogues independent.
- Approve privacy, terms, taxes, contact route, retention and benefit/referral rules.
- Configure private persistent hosting, backups, TLS and founder authentication. Run a restore rehearsal.
- Run controlled Google booking/cancellation, email/resume and Sheets tests with founder-owned destinations. Test WhatsApp only after template/permission confirmation. Record actual provider IDs and outcomes privately.
- Check mobile, keyboard, errors, unavailable providers and the exact approved PDF/HTML proposal on the production hostname.

## Modest initial promotion

- Use a founder networking link such as `/quote?utm_source=networking&utm_medium=founder&utm_campaign=pilot`.
- Use separate referral and partner links; do not expose a referred client's contact information to the referrer.
- Prepare an organic LinkedIn post about the free diagnostic → guided scope → free consultation → provisional proposal process.
- Prepare an Instagram project-planning walkthrough with real process screenshots and no invented results.
- Invite existing contacts only through a separately authorized communication step. This build sends no campaign messages.
- Explain eligible-fee promotion exclusions. Do not advertise gated prices, false urgency or guaranteed leads/revenue.

## Measure the first cohort

Review registration and discovery completion, booking-gate drop-off, confirmed/attended consultations, quote unlocks, requests, approved proposals, acceptance and verified first payment. Compare cost per qualified consultation and paid conversion using actual campaign expenditure recorded outside this app. Do not change the booking gate through an experiment without founder approval.
